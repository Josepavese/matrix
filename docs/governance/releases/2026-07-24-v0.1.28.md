This release isolates cancellation of concurrent ACP runs that share one
agent/workspace client. Failed or cancelled routes now drain the shared client
until every active conversation lease has finished, while run cancellation
targets the run-bound remote session before cancelling its local context. It
closes
`issues/closed/2026-07-24-codex-acp-concurrent-run-cancel-poisons-sibling.md`.
