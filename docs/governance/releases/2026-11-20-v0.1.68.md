# Matrix v0.1.68

- A current vault records its value format after the first verification. The
  daemon publishes its initialization state and keeps ownership through broker
  startup. [Issue #22 and measurements](../../../issues/closed/2026-11-20-vault-startup-broker.md)
- ACP model-selection failures report the requested model, negotiated version,
  actual method and peer response. The reported worker's deployment
  qualification is tracked by its owner. [Issue #23 and verification](../../../issues/closed/2026-11-20-acp-model-preflight-diagnostics.md)

The Linux candidate passed the deployment preflight, including lint,
governance, race tests, build and GoReleaser configuration. After the final
explicit unlock adjustment, the affected PAL and broker race tests passed.
The integration evidence linked above uses the real daemon and official
OpenCode binaries with isolated homes and a synthetic inference provider.
